📘 Builder Guide
1. The Pixel Maker

Go to the 🖍️ Pixel Maker tab. Draw a simple sword or food item using the tools. Click Export when done!

2. Texture Upload

Go to 🎨 Texture Upload. Click the button to upload the PNG you just drew so the builder can access it.

3. Logic Registration

Go to 🧱 Logic. Under 'Custom Items', drag 'item_create_sword' into the workspace. Name it 'mod:my_sword'.

4. Export Mod

Click the purple 'Export Texture .ZIP' button on the top bar to pack your textures for Eaglercraft!
